# LiveAskew and Bee homepage brief

Self-authored, not interviewed. The client delegated the ScrollCraft direction after establishing the brand, product hierarchy and desired tone.

## Core idea

LiveAskew is the company. Bee is its first and most important product: an intelligent stylist that listens to the whole person before suggesting clothes.

The page should feel warm, alive, clever, stylish and human. It must be more energetic and approachable than a conventional luxury site while still feeling considered. Avoid beige luxury grids, identical product cards, faceless technology language and generic AI glow.

## Journey and feeling curve

1. LiveAskew presents Bee with a direct, high-energy opening.
2. Establish LiveAskew as the parent company.
3. Reveal what Bee notices through a scroll-linked style thread.
4. Reach the emotional peak when fragments of real life become a clear style identity.
5. Show the practical wardrobe outcome.
6. Introduce The Edit, The Shop and The Studio as distinct LiveAskew products.
7. Resolve with a warm invitation to begin Bee's interview.

Seen -> curious -> understood -> delighted -> confident -> invited.

The peak is the style north star reveal. The authored silence immediately before it comes from the accumulating fragments and extra vertical space.

## Page grammar and signature move

Chaptered editorial with one continuous scroll-linked thread. Each chapter has its own composition and ground, with hard cuts between scenes instead of repeated cards.

The style thread gathers personal fragments as the visitor scrolls, then resolves into a vivid style identity. It represents Bee connecting life context into something coherent.

## Range and visual language

Editorial and playful. Warm but not soft. Bold coral, electric indigo and acidic chartreuse are held together by ink and paper. Existing LiveAskew fashion photography and the Bee orb remain central. Typography carries editorial confidence, while layouts use collage, stamps, hard rules and asymmetric spacing.

## Thesis

It's the site where your life becomes your style.
