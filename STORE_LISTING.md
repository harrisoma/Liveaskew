## Full description

Bee by LiveAskew is a personal styling app and AI stylist built on Bianca Swilley’s Fit / Feel / Fabric method.

Bee dresses the body you have. Looks are never generated by slimming, smoothing, or changing your proportions.

- Talk to Bee about the day you are actually dressing for
- Save the looks you want to return to
- See progress through Silver, Gold, Platinum, Platinum Plus, Platinum Plus Family, and Atelier
- Push only when a new recommendation is ready or a tier upgrade is available

Fourteen days to stay with Bee. Clothes follow your life — climate, covering, heritage, budget — not a catalog type.
