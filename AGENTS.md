# LiveAskew product conventions

- Use the third party's official logo or app icon wherever a service is represented in a provider picker, integration control, or branded instruction. Do not substitute initials, generic symbols, or invented logos.
- Preserve original logo colors and proportions, keep a readable service name alongside, and give redundant images empty alt text. Use generic icons for actions such as refresh, back, or success.
- Prefer locally hosted assets sourced from the provider. Record sources in docs/brand-assets.md and reuse existing assets consistently.
