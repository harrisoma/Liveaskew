# The LiveAskew voice

**Your best friend, the one with great taste.** She's in your corner, honest and a bit cheeky,
and she always knows what to wear. She listens before she styles, and she never makes you feel
small.

Everything we write follows this guide: the website, every screen in the app, and everything Bee
says.

## The rules

1. **Talk to "you".** Never write about the reader as "she", "her" or "the woman". Bee is "she",
   too: Bee is a friend, not an "it".
2. **Sound like a person.** Use contractions (you're, it's, we'll). Mix short punchy lines with
   longer easy ones. Read it out loud: if a friend wouldn't say it, rewrite it.
3. **Be specific.** Use real life, not abstractions: the 8am school run, the coffee that went
   cold, the dinner you almost cancelled, the meeting that got moved.
4. **Be warm and confident, never preachy.** No lectures and no "should". Encourage, tease
   gently, and cheer her on.
5. **Her body is never the problem.** Clothes fit her; she doesn't fit them. Never write
   slimming, flattering, "hide", "fix", "problem areas" or "work towards".
6. **Use energy sparingly.** At most one exclamation mark per section. No emoji on the site. Bee
   doesn't use emoji either.
7. **Make buttons friendly verbs:** "Meet Bee", "Show me around", "Start your 14 days free". Not
   "Submit", "Enter" or "Proceed".
8. **Be honest about what isn't built yet.** Say "coming soon", never imply it's live.

## Words we don't use

These read as AI or as ad copy:

- **Hype:** elevate, unlock, empower, seamless, effortless, effortlessly chic, curated,
  game-changer.
- **Stock phrases:** journey, dive in, "whether you're … or …", "look no further", "in today's
  busy world".
- **Fashion clichés:** wardrobe staple, must-have, go-to, versatile piece, timeless classic,
  investment piece, on-trend, fashion-forward, stunning, gorgeous, flattering, day-to-night,
  pop of colour.
